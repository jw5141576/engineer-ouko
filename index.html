THE JOURNEY • SINCE 2022

Built Through Grit, Resourcefulness & Purpose

My path into software engineering wasn't traditional, but it forged how I build today. In 2022, I was awarded a scholarship by the African Leadership University (ALU). With scarce hardware resources, unreliable internet, and frequent power outages, learning to code became an exercise in pure adaptability teaching me to engineer lightweight, resilient systems built to perform even under real-world constraints.

Determined to master my craft, I built my web foundations from scratch through Alison before advancing into formal Software Engineering training at Media Crest College. Today, I combine that hands-on technical execution with Business Administration studies at University of the People allowing me to bridge the gap between clean code and measurable business ROI.

"I have a profound passion for growing and uplifting others. Technology is not just about writing clean logic or setting up servers it is a tool for empowerment, ensuring that as the digital world moves forward, no business or community gets left behind."
